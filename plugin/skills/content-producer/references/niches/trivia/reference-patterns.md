## RP01 — Octopus surprises

- Pattern: Curious Mind POST-003.
- Topic: `animals`.
- Format: `single_subject_fast`.
- Profile: `fast_trivia`.
- Config: 7 facts, `plain_lines`, `en-US`, no emoji.
  Honor the user's actual config instead of copying this count.

### Canva copy

```text
Did you know?

An octopus's arms can taste what they touch.

Three hearts keep blood moving through an octopus.

Oxygen-rich octopus blood is blue because it contains copper.

Octopuses can change their skin's texture, not just color.

Veined octopuses carry coconut shells to use as shelters.

Some octopuses solve mazes to reach food.

Giant Pacific octopuses can tell individual keepers apart.

Follow for more strange animal facts.
```

### Editorial annotation

- Opening: Taste through arms is concrete before any anatomy lesson.
- Middle: Move through organs, chemistry, appearance and tools; do not group all anatomy first.
- Closing: Keeper recognition adds a human connection distinct from maze-solving.
- CTA: Follow promises another animal discovery.
- Limits not inherited: Do not inherit the dataset's unsupported extreme abilities, nine literal brains, or universal human-like motives.

## Anti-examples and complete-card revisions

Before fixtures are deliberately weak, synthetic teaching material, never
factual/source evidence or saved private audit dumps. BA01 has no invented
quantities; BA04 repeats one supported proposition and must fail dedup/selection.
Other before lines are style exercises based on the supported after claims.
Do not copy their technical wording or turn their defects into new requirements.
Each After link resolves to the complete revised card above, including all facts
and CTA; read both whole cards and the stated selection decision.

### BA01 — Repeated measurements

Before — reject or revise:

```text
Did you know?

American alligators can be measured by snout width.

American crocodiles can be measured by snout width.

American alligators can be measured by body length.

American crocodiles can be measured by body length.

American crocodilians can be measured in feet.

Which measurement surprised you?
```

After: [RP11 complete card](#rp11--american-gator-or-crocodile).

Decision: Replace an empty specification table with five meaningful paired differences. The before fixture deliberately avoids invented numbers; giving each row a different number would not repair its missing reveal.

### BA02 — Weak opening

```text
Did you know?
```

### BA03 — Jargon hides the reveal

```text
Did you know?

Octopuses possess a tripartite cardiac arrangement.

Copper-containing hemocyanin facilitates oxygen transport.

Papillae enable integumentary texture modulation.

```

After: [RP01 complete card](#rp01--octopus-surprises).

Decision: Use familiar verbs, lead with taste and color, keep species qualifiers, and change the lifeless CTA. The after is a complete collection, not a list of synonym substitutions.
